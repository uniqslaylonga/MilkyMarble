ABOUT US

About Milky Marble

Milky Marble is a Jelly Milk product business that offers customizable products through an online ordering platform. Our website allows customers to browse available products, customize their orders, add items to their cart, make payments, monitor their order status, and schedule pickup.

Milky Marble operates primarily through pre-orders during designated ordering periods, while maintaining a limited amount of ready stock for last-minute orders. Customers can choose from available products and customization options provided by the system. Orders are prepared by the production team and are collected through the designated pickup process.
